# CC Herbarium — A Grass-Type Herbarium

Welcome to the herbarium. Every Grass-type Pokémon on record is kept here as a specimen, labelled with the plant biology or genetics its design echoes. In the back rooms, a set of Case Files holds open-access research up against individual specimens to see how far the science actually reaches. Please don't tap on the glass.

**[▶ Enter the herbarium](https://coolcatplayground.github.io/herbarium/)** — open daily, on phone and desktop. Admission is free.

## A guide to the rooms

- **The Glasshouse** — the full living collection, delivered fresh from PokéAPI. Search by name or browse by habitat. Habitats are assigned from each specimen's secondary type (Grass/Water is filed as wetland flora, Grass/Dark as nocturnal-function flora, and so on). Where that rule misfiles a specimen, the curator corrects the label by hand.
- **Specimen sheets** — one sheet per Pokémon: stats, Pokédex flavor text, a hand-written botanical field note (133 on file so far), a growth-stage timeline, and an evolution tree that keeps its branches instead of being pruned into a straight line. Alternate forms (Megas, regional variants, size forms) share their base species' note and say so openly. The herbarium does not forge labels.
- **The Determination Key** — for visitors who find an unknown specimen and want to name it. In the tradition of a printed botanical key, answer one character at a time (habitat affinity, evolutionary stage, generation, form) and the candidates narrow until one remains. Some steps branch more than two ways, and any earlier answer can be changed without starting over.
- **The Grafting Bench** — three Case Files, each pairing a specimen with published research and a model you can play with:
  - *Roselia's Flower Pigment* — a gene-expression console for adjusting anthocyanin intensity across three pathway stages, plus a clearly labelled speculative wing on how a blue flower might happen.
  - *Cacnea's Water Economy* — a drought simulation. Build a cactus from actual cactus body types, advance the calendar, and watch it shift through CAM, CAM idling and, eventually, tissue collapse. There are two ways to die and exactly one build that survives until the rains.
  - *Vileplume's Toxic Pollen* — three independently evolved strategies insects use to survive a toxic plant, each with its citation and a check against where they appear in the games. (Vileplume is the final form of Specimen No. 001. The curator is not biased. The curator is extremely biased.)
- **The Reading Room** — 15 verified open-access papers, each cross-referenced to the specimens and Case Files it supports.
- **The Propagation Bench** — speculative Grass-type species, grown from plant science in the greenhouse out back.

## Construction

The herbarium stood unbuilt for years for lack of carpenters. The curator had the botany, the labels and the stubbornness, but no one to do the carpentry. It was finally raised with the help of Mr. AI Carpenter. The concept, the biology behind each specimen and the curation are the curator's; the code was built with generative AI under the curator's direction. Every paper in the Reading Room is an open-access publication.

Specimen sprites and type icons come from PokéAPI's sprite repository and are fetched at build time. No other game assets are redistributed.
