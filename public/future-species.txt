=====================================================
FOLIA CODEX — THE PROPAGATION BENCH (FUTURE CULTIVARS)
=====================================================
This is a plain text file. Open and edit it with Notepad, TextEdit, or
any text editor — no coding required.

This file is for speculative Grass-type concepts of your own — Pokémon
that don't exist yet, imagined as a plausible next step from real,
current plant science or genetics research. Think of it as a nursery:
cuttings and ideas waiting to grow into a full concept, not finished
specimens.

Unlike field-notes.txt, there's no PokéAPI data backing these — no dex
number, no real sprite, no stats. Every entry renders as a "?" placeholder
card until you replace it with your own idea (and, eventually, maybe
actual artwork).

HOW TO ADD ONE:
1. Copy the TEMPLATE block below and paste it anywhere in this file.
2. Change "template" on the first line to a short unique id (lowercase,
   no spaces — e.g. "crispr-drought-cultivar").
3. Fill in what fields you can. "name" can just be "???" if you haven't
   named it yet — that's fine, it's meant to support half-formed ideas.
4. Set "types" to whatever type(s) you're designing for — this is a real
   creative decision here, not pulled from any database like it is for
   actual specimens. One type or two (e.g. "Grass" or "Bug / Ghost").
5. Optional: set "related_manuscript" to the id of an entry in
   manuscripts.txt (see public/manuscripts.txt) to link this concept back
   to the real paper that inspired it.
6. Optional: if you have drawn a concept sheet, drop <id>-concept.png in
   the repo root, run scripts\encode-concept.ps1, and set "art" to the
   path it prints (e.g. "concepts/ant-fungi.jpg"). Concepts without a
   sheet render perfectly well - the bench does not assume one exists.
6. Save the file. Refresh the app in your browser to see it appear.

### ant-fungi-pact-concept   [DRAFT - please review]
name: Myrmycel (working name)
types: Bug / Ghost
inspired_by: Ophiocordyceps unilateralis and its carpenter-ant host, read against the far older ant-fungus partnerships that are not parasitic at all
concept: One concept in two bodies, not two Pokemon. A Camponotus-style carpenter ant and a living hyphal organism that bond by consent and move as one - the hyphae draping the ant like living cloth, mushrooms breaking through the folds. The Ghost typing is what the pairing looks like from outside: a body moving to a rhythm that is no longer only its own. But the bond is temporary and either side can end it. Its ability, Symbiotic Sync, powers the pair up while HP is high and lets them down as it falls, and the hidden ability, Reunion, means that when the pair faints the partner does not die with it - it stays behind and can be found again. Separated, they are simply an ant and a fungus, both alive.
real_basis: Two real things, and the concept sits between them on purpose. Ophiocordyceps unilateralis really does hijack Camponotus ants down to the level of gene expression, forcing a death grip at a particular height and humidity before killing the host and fruiting from its head - that is where the imagery comes from, and it is genuinely one-sided. But ants and fungi also run one of the oldest mutualisms on the planet: leafcutter and other attine ants have farmed fungus for something like fifty-five million years, each side now unable to live without the other and neither in charge. This concept takes its look from the parasite and its terms from the mutualism, and the swap is the invention - the departure from the science, stated rather than hidden.
related_manuscript: debekker-2015-zombie-ant-genomics
art: concepts/ant-fungi.jpg
### END
